On Sunday, August 2, Archbishop Paul S. Coakley visited St. Ann in Elgin and joined parishioners from across our Tri-Parish Catholic Community.

Before Mass, Archbishop Coakley blessed the new Conference & Media Room, dedicated in loving memory of our beloved former pastor, Fr. Linh Bui. The room will support parish meetings, formation, communication, and service to the community.

Archbishop Coakley then celebrated Holy Mass with our parish family. We are grateful for his pastoral visit, for the priests who joined us, and for everyone who helped prepare this joyful day.
