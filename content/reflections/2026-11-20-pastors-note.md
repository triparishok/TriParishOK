It has been a year since I took up the Pastoral care of this Tri-Parish. I am so happy to be your Pastor. We as a Tri-Parish have done many improvements in the past year.

Thank you for all your support in taking care of our Tri-Parish.

As we begin the new liturgical year with Advent, we have an opportunity to spend more time with Jesus by participating in the daily Masses and Adoration.

During Advent all weekday Masses (Mon-Sat) will be Celebrated in the morning followed by and hour of Adoration. No evening Adorations. I invite all of you to take this wonderful oppotunity to spend more time with Jesus during this season of Advent.

As we celebrate the Solemnity of Our Lord Jesus Christ, King of the Universe, the Gospel reminds us that Jesus is a King who reigns through love, compassion, and service. He identifies Himself with the hungry, the thirsty, the stranger, the sick, and those in need. Jesus tells us,iWhatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.i These words remind us that our love for God must be reflected in how we treat others. Every act of kindness, generosity, forgiveness, and compassion becomes an expression of our love for Christ.

As we bring the liturgical year to a close, let us ask ourselves whether we truly recognize Jesus in those around us. May Christ our King reign in our hearts and inspire us to serve Him by loving one another, especially those most in need. Amen.

Yours in Christ,

Fr. Deva
