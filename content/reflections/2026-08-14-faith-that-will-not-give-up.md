Her persistence teaches us something important about prayer. Sometimes we pray and do not receive the answer we expect—or we may feel as though God is silent. Yet the Canaanite woman continues to trust. She believes that even a small portion of God’s mercy is enough.

Jesus ultimately praises her, saying, **“O woman, great is your faith!”** Her faith was not based on getting an immediate answer. It was a faith that trusted God even in the midst of uncertainty. How often do we become discouraged when our prayers seem unanswered? This Gospel reminds us to keep bringing our needs, our families, and our struggles to the Lord. God hears the prayer of a heart that continues to seek Him.

The Canaanite woman also reminds us that God’s mercy is not limited by human boundaries. As the first reading tells us, God’s house is to be **“a house of prayer for all peoples.”** We are called to be a Church that welcomes, prays for, and reaches out to everyone. 

This week, may we pray with the Canaanite woman’s faith: **“Lord, help me.”** And may we trust that, even when we cannot see the answer, God’s mercy is always at work.
