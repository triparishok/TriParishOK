description: In this Sunday's Gospel, Jesus reminds us through the Parable of
  the Talents that everything we have is a gift from God. Our talents, time,
  abilities, and resources are entrusted to us not to be hidden, but to be
  shared in loving service to others.
draft: true

Sometimes fear or insecurity keeps us from using the gifts God has given us. Yet the Lord does not ask us to be perfect or to have more than others. He simply asks us to be faithful and generous with what we have.

As we prepare for the end of the liturgical year, let us reflect on how we are using God's gifts. May we have the courage to share our blessings, serve others, and live each day with faithfulness and love, so that we, too, may hear the words:

"Well done, my good and faithful servant."
